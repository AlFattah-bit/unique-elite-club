OUR COMMITMENT

Club rules

Members should follow the club's approved constitution and decisions.

  • Physical club meetings are held on Sundays, according to the club schedule.
  • New members must attend physically before being formally recognized.
  • Three consecutive missed meetings may result in temporary WhatsApp suspension under club rules.
  • Members should follow agreed payment schedules and applicable late-payment rules.
  • Regular savings and optional investment activities are recorded separately.

These are a website summary. The club's formally approved rules take precedence.
